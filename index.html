Privacy Policy for FlareLog: Migraine Tracker

Last updated: October 4, 2026

FlareLog ("we", "our") is designed as a privacy-first health tracking application.

1. Data We Collect & How It Is Used

  • Health & Symptom Data: Headache frequency, pain intensity, duration, symptoms, and self-reported medication logs. This data is used solely to generate observational pattern charts and clinical reports for the user.
  • Personal Information: If you choose to create an optional cloud account via Google Sign-In or Email, we store your email address and authentication identifier in Supabase to sync your records.

2. Data Storage & Security

Your records reside locally on your device in an offline database. When optional cloud sync is enabled, data in transit is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. We do not sell or monetize personal health records, nor do we share them with third-party ad networks.

3. Data Deletion

You can delete all local and cloud data at any time inside the app via Settings & Privacy > Delete All Data.

4. Medical Disclaimer

FlareLog is not a medical device and does not provide diagnostic or therapeutic advice. Always seek professional care for severe or concerning symptoms.

5. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries: support@flarelog.app
